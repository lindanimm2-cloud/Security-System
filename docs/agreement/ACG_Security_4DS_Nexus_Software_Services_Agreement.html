Agreement Overview & Definitions

1.1 Purpose. 4DS Solutions will provide ACG Security with a configurable digital security technology platform connecting clients, the control room, field officers, technicians, supervisors and, where selected, medical/ambulance operations. The platform is based on the 4DS Nexus technology stack with role-specific portals and a central API/data layer.

1.2 Contract principle. The platform is modular. ACG Security is not required to purchase every feature. The Owner or authorised Client representative must select either ALL FEATURES or individual modules in Schedule 22. Only selected features, together with the applicable commercial option, form part of the contracted scope.

1.3 Definitions.

“Platform” means the ACG Security implementation of the 4DS Nexus technology stack.

“Feature Schedule” means Schedule 22.

“Client” means ACG Security.

“Provider” means 4DS Solutions (Pty) Ltd.

“Go-Live” means the date on which the agreed production scope is made available for operational use.

“Change Request” means a written request for work outside the selected scope.

1.4 Precedence. If there is a conflict, the signed Feature Schedule and Commercial Schedule prevail over general descriptions, demonstrations, mock-ups or earlier discussions to the extent of the conflict.

Scope & Feature Selection

3.1 Owner choice. Before implementation, the Owner/authorised representative must complete Schedule 22 by selecting ALL FEATURES, CUSTOM SELECTION or PHASED SELECTION. Where ALL FEATURES is selected, all modules listed in Schedule 22 are included, subject to exclusions, dependencies, third-party costs and agreed commercial limits.

3.2 Individual selection. If ALL FEATURES is not selected, every required module must be ticked. Unticked modules are expressly excluded from the initial scope and may be added later through a Change Request, revised quotation or written addendum.

3.3 Dependencies. Some features depend on third-party APIs, devices, CCTV systems, alarm panels, mapping services, payment services, mobile operating systems, app-store approvals or other infrastructure. Integration remains subject to credentials, technical compatibility, approvals, usage charges and provider availability.

3.4 Commercial logic. The price schedule is intentionally modular. ACG may purchase only the components required. The individual reference prices provide a transparent value breakdown; the ALL FEATURES package receives a separate strategic bundle price.

Confidentiality & Privacy

18.2 Permitted disclosure. Disclosure may be made to employees, professional advisers, subcontractors or service providers who require the information and are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations, or where disclosure is required by law.

18.4 Security incidents. Each party shall notify the other within a reasonable period after becoming aware of a material security incident affecting the other party's confidential information or contracted platform, subject to lawful and operational constraints.

18.5 Survival. Confidentiality obligations survive termination for so long as the information remains confidential, subject to applicable law.

Warranties, Liability & Service Limitations

19.1 Professional performance. The Provider will perform the contracted services with reasonable skill and care.

19.2 No absolute guarantee. The Platform is a technology system and cannot guarantee that every incident will be detected, that every response will succeed, that a security event will be prevented, or that third-party services will always be available.

19.3 Client operational responsibility. ACG Security remains responsible for its physical security operations, officer conduct, licensing, dispatch decisions, emergency procedures, equipment maintenance and compliance obligations unless expressly transferred by written agreement.

19.4 Third parties. The Provider is not responsible for outages, policy changes, API changes, approval delays, pricing changes or failures of third-party providers outside its reasonable control.

19.5 Limitation. The parties should agree a reasonable cap on liability in the Commercial Schedule, with appropriate exclusions for matters that cannot lawfully be limited. Independent legal advice is recommended before final signature.

19.6 Insurance. Each party remains responsible for maintaining insurance appropriate to its business and contractual responsibilities, unless otherwise agreed.
