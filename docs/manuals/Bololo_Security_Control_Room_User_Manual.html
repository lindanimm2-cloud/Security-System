01

Your seat in the room

You must

  • See new panic / fire / medical in seconds
  • Assign the nearest available officer
  • Talk to client and unit — and log it
  • Keep silent panics quiet on the phone

You must not

  • Leave a critical unassigned
  • Close “probably false” with no check
  • Paste client addresses into group chats
  • Work under someone else’s name

The system is a tool. Radio discipline and Bololo SOP still win.

Desk
Two screens if you can

Ops Board + Live Map. CCTV on the second screen when a site is live.

Login
Your name on the header

Control Panel · /login. Never the previous operator’s session.

Radio
SOP still wins

The app logs. The radio moves units. Use both.

04

Panic path

1Alert
2Open job
3Map
4Assign
5Talk
6Stay
7Log
1

Incident hits Ops Board — open it immediately.

2

Read type, silent vs audible, name, address.

3

Live Map: client pin + nearest available officer.

4

Dispatch → assign (or Emergency notify only if SOP says all-call).

5

Call / chat the client only if it is safe — not on silent panic.

6

Stay until En route, then On scene. Write every step on the timeline.

S
Silent panic

Do not phone the premises in a way that could expose the client. Dispatch, watch CCTV/map, follow Bololo SOP.

Dispatch, CCTV & chat

Dispatch
Assign / reassign

If they do not Accept, give it to the next unit. Do not wait hoping.

Unit vs pin

06

Handover

Pass on
Still open

Panics, units on scene, CCTV sites dark, angry / VIP waiting.

Map
Grey or empty

Use incident list + radio. Do not freeze.

GPS
Officer missing

Call them. Do not invent a position.

Assign
Nobody free

Escalate to supervisor / owner immediately.

1

Walk the incoming operator through every OPEN critical — name, unit, last timeline line.

2

Show cameras that are dark and any VIP / angry client still waiting.

3

Confirm they can see their own name on the header, then you sign out.

07

Shift discipline

Good log

  • Assigned O-24 14:02
  • En route 14:04 · ETA 6 min
  • On scene 14:11 · resident on site
  • False alarm · wind on sensor · resolved

Useless log

  • “Told him on WhatsApp”
  • Status still OPEN at 06:00
  • No officer name
  • Silent panic called on loudspeaker
✓
End of night

No OPEN critical without a named next action. The morning owner scan depends on you.
