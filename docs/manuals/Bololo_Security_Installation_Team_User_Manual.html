The eight statuses

Move forward in order. Control Room and the owner can see this bar.

Truth
En route means you left

Office uses this like a tracker. Lying breaks dispatch trust.

Stuck
No access?

Photo the gate, note it, chat office. Never Complete.

Done

04

On the road & on site

1

Job → Navigate (maps). Advance En route when you pull off, Arrived at the gate.

2

Walk the site. Confirm power, mounting, and that the van kit matches the job card.

3

Tick the checklist as you go — it is the proof pack.

Install checklist

01–02
Kit & access

Equipment on the van. Site access confirmed.

03–04
Mount & NVR

Cameras up. Recorder connected.

05–06
Network & cameras

LAN/Wi‑Fi. Channels named for Control Room.

07–10
Test, demo, sign

Recording, remote view, client demo, sign-off.
