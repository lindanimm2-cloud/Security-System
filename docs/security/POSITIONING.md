# 4DS Field Operations — Positioning

## Approved product language

Use this positioning in product copy, proposals, and capability packs:

> **4DS Field Operations — Secure, auditable, resilient field operations software designed for high-assurance security environments.**

Supporting phrases that are accurate when controls exist:

- Secure, auditable field operations
- Designed for high-assurance security environments
- Built toward government / critical-operations readiness
- Evidence-aware incident and patrol workflows

## Do not claim

Do **not** market or label the product as:

- “Military-grade”
- “Government certified” / “ISO 27001 certified” / “NIST certified”
- Guaranteed RPO/RTO numbers
- “Unhackable” or similar absolute security claims

…unless you hold current, verifiable certifications, contracts, assessments, or measured recovery tests that support the claim.

Certification and assessment are separate from implementing controls. ISO/IEC 27001 and NIST CSF 2.0 are **roadmaps and frameworks**, not badges you attach by writing features alone.

## Compliance posture (roadmap, not certification)

| Reference | Role for 4DS |
|-----------|----------------|
| **POPIA** (SA) | Appropriate technical and organisational measures for personal information; risk identification; safeguards; verification; continual update |
| **NIST CSF 2.0** | Govern → Identify → Protect → Detect → Respond → Recover mapping for product and ops |
| **ISO/IEC 27001** | Long-term ISMS target after controls and evidence exist |
| **National Archives / records guidance** | Integrity, audit trails, retention for operational records where applicable |

Demo / local development modes are **not** high-assurance environments. Never present demo behaviour as production assurance.

## Principle

The UI should communicate seriousness. Architecture, access control, auditability, evidence integrity, testing, documentation, and operational resilience are what make the system high-assurance.
